## 🌠 What This Project Is

**Star Wars Jedi: Survivor 2** arrived in 2026 with a sprawling galaxy, denser enemy encounters, and a narrative that rewards patience. But not every player has forty hours a week to spend grinding through Force echoes and bounty hunter ambushes.

This repository hosts a **standalone companion utility** — a trainer environment that runs alongside your game session and offers granular toggles for the systems you want to soften. Nothing more, nothing less. It is a **single-player experience modifier**, built for offline enjoyment, streamer-friendly showcases, and accessibility-minded players who find certain encounters physically or cognitively demanding.

Think of it as a **difficulty slider that the developers forgot to include** — one that goes from "padawan" all the way to "unbothered deity strolling through the Outer Rim."

### 🎯 Who Is This For?

- **Narrative-first players** who want to experience the story without repeated combat resets.
- **Accessibility advocates** who need lower cognitive load during boss fights.
- **Content creators** who need reliable, repeatable footage for guides and reviews.
- **Speedrunners in training** who want to practice routing without dying to the same rancor seventeen times.
- **Modders and tinkerers** who enjoy peeking under the hood of a living game.

## 🧘 A Note on Philosophy

We believe that **how you play is your business**. Some players treat difficulty as a badge of honor. Others treat it as a wall. This project exists for the second group — and for the first group on their off days.

The trainer does **not** touch online services, does **not** interact with multiplayer sessions, and does **not** modify save files permanently unless you explicitly tell it to. It is a **runtime overlay** — a whisper in the ear of the game, not a rewrite of its soul.

## ⚔️ Core Feature Matrix

Below is the heart of the suite. Each module can be toggled independently, and each persists its state between sessions unless reset manually.

| Module | Emoji | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **God Mode** | 🛡️ | Nullifies incoming damage from sabers, blasters, creatures, and environmental hazards. You still take fall damage — we are merciful, not careless. |
| **Infinite Force** | 🌊 | Force meter never depletes. Push, pull, mind trick, and slow to your heart's content. |
| **One-Hit Kills** | ⚡ | Every successful strike ends the encounter. Works on standard enemies, elites, and most bosses. Dismemberment toggles remain vanilla. |
| **Unlimited XP** | 📈 | Skill points accumulate without cap. Invest in every tree, respec freely, and never agonize over a build again. |
| **Unlimited Credits** | 💰 | Vendors stop counting your wallet. Buy every poncho, every hilt, every cosmetic the galaxy offers. |
| **Force Slow Mastery** | 🕰️ | Extends the slow window and reduces its cooldown to nearly nothing. |
| **Instant Cooldowns** | 🔄 | Stance abilities, stims, and special moves ready on demand. |
| **No Fall Damage** | 🪂 | Jump from any ledge, any height. The galaxy's gravity becomes a suggestion. |
| **Enemy Freeze** | ❄️ | Pause AI behavior in a configurable radius — useful for screenshots and cinematic capture. |
| **Stealth Aura** | 🫥 | Enemies lose track of you after a few seconds of breaking line of sight. |
| **Loot Magnet** | 🧲 | Collectibles and dropped items pull toward you within a generous range. |

### 🧩 How Modules Interact

Modules are **orthogonal by design** — you can stack them without conflicts. However, we recommend pairing **God Mode** with **One-Hit Kills** cautiously, as the combination can trivialize encounters to the point of removing all tension. That is your call to make, not ours.

## 🔧 Configuration Deep Dive

The suite stores its state in a plain-text configuration file with a human-readable syntax. No binary blobs, no obfuscation — you can edit it in any text editor.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: The overlay does not appear.**
A: Ensure the game is running in **borderless windowed** or **windowed** mode. Exclusive fullscreen can block overlay rendering on some GPU drivers.

**Q: My anti-cheat flagged the suite.**
A: The suite is designed for **single-player, offline sessions only**. Do not use it in any environment with anti-cheat active. See the Disclaimer section below.

**Q: Modules reset after a game update.**
A: This is expected. Game patches often change memory layouts. Check the repository wiki for a compatibility note, and update the suite when a new release lands.

**Q: Can I share my preset with a friend?**
A: Absolutely. Profiles are portable text files. Share them freely.

**Q: Is there a mobile companion app?**
A: Not at this time. The overlay is desktop-native by design.

**Q: Does the suite collect telemetry?**
A: No. The suite is **fully offline** and sends nothing anywhere. Ever.
